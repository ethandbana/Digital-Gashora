Welcome to Digital Gashora

We are teaching computer skills to children in Gashora Centre, Bugesera. With limited resources but unlimited determination, we believe every child deserves a chance to learn digital skills.

Currently run by a Level 4 Computer Systems and Architecture student from ESSA Nyarugungaso, our program is hands-on, practical, and built for real learning.

"Technology opens opportunities. We open the door."

— Digital Gashora

About Digital Gashora

Who we are: We are a small but passionate computer literacy program based in Gashora Centre, Bugesera. What started as an internship has become a mission to bring digital skills to children who have limited access to computers.

Our story: I am a Level 4 Computer Systems student at ESSA Nyarugungaso. During my holiday internship, I was given the opportunity to manage Digital Gashora. With one computer and a group of eager learners, we are building something real.

Our challenge: Limited computers. Mixed skill levels. But we don't wait for perfect conditions — we teach with what we have.
